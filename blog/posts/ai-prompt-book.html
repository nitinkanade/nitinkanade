Introducing AI Prompt Book: Copy & Remix AI Art Prompts on Android

Published on July 9, 2026 • By Nitin Kanade

Stop Guessing Prompts. Start Creating Masterpieces.

If you have spent any time with tools like Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, DALL-E, or Gemini, you know the routine. You type an idea, wait 30 seconds, and get back something that looks slightly weird, overly distorted, or completely off-topic. Then begins the tedious dance of "prompt engineering"—swapping words, adding technical tags, tweaking lighting styles, and restarting the generation over and over.

As a developer who loves building utility apps under my ni18 handle, I realized that the hardest part of generative AI art isn't the technology—it’s finding the right words. Most of us end up saving random text strings in our native notes app, cluttering our clipboards, or juggling multiple browser tabs.

I wanted a sandbox utility that makes this workflow seamless on mobile. That’s why I built and launched AI Prompt Book.

"AI Prompt Book is a free, privacy-first Android app designed to simplify your AI art workflow. It provides a curated library of high-quality, tested prompts that you can copy in a single tap or remix with your own photo."

What Makes AI Prompt Book Different?

There are plenty of prompt directories on the web, but they are often cluttered, heavy, ad-choked, or built exclusively for desktop. When designing AI Prompt Book, I applied the same design guidelines that govern my other offline-first applications: make it blazing fast, incredibly simple, and respectful of user privacy.

1. Curated, Tested, and Copy-Ready Prompts

Every prompt inside the app is hand-tested against popular generators (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, DALL-E, and Gemini). You won't find broken configurations or generic formulas. We group prompts into easy-to-browse categories:

  • Anime: Perfect keywords for authentic illustrations, cell-shading, and retro styles.
  • Fantasy: Magical lighting, mythical beasts, and sweeping landscapes.
  • Wallpapers: High-resolution background layouts for mobile and desktop.
  • Logos & Icons: Minimalist, vector-style prompts for builders and creators.
  • Characters: Highly detailed cinematic subject renders.

2. The Killer Feature: Photo Remix

Have you ever seen an incredible AI style and wished you could place yourself or your own subject inside it? With Photo Remix, you can.

Simply pick a style prompt from our library, upload a photo from your gallery, and the app pairs the style structure with your subject. You can recreate trending looks—like turning yourself into a 3D clay figurine, an anime character, or a cinematic film protagonist—with a single tap.

3. One-Tap Copy & Instant Workflows

Nobody wants to copy-paste line by line or switch back and forth between apps. AI Prompt Book lets you copy the entire prompt structure instantly, allowing you to paste it into Discord (for Midjourney), ChatGPT (for DALL-E), or your Stable Diffusion dashboard without losing your rhythm.

4. Offline Favorites & Privacy-First Abstraction

True to the ni18 engineering philosophy:

  • Offline Access: When you mark prompts as favorites, their metadata and preview images are cached locally on your device. You can browse, review, and plan your prompting sessions even when you are on a train with zero network connection.
  • Privacy by Default: We don't require an account, email address, or social log-in. Your gallery photos used in the Remix feature are processed locally or sandboxed safely. What happens in your app, stays in your app.

5. Dynamic Over-the-Air (OTA) Updates

Generative models change quickly. Instead of forcing you to download a new APK from the Play Store every time a new version of Midjourney or DALL-E drops, I built an OTA prompt delivery pipeline. New prompts, keywords, and style categories are pushed to your app automatically in the background.

The Companion Website & Prompting Guides

To support the mobile app, I also launched an interactive web hub: aipromptbook.ni18.in.

Even if you don't have your Android device nearby, the website serves as a valuable resource. It features in-depth prompting guides written by our team to help you level up your creative output:

  • Negative Prompts Guide: Learn how to use negative prompts to clean up extra fingers, distorted text, and messy backgrounds.
  • Anime AI Keywords: The lighting terms, camera angles, and stylistic terms that actually matter for authentic artwork.
  • Stunning 4K Wallpapers: Copy-ready wallpaper setups and structural parameters.

How to Get Started

Ready to take your AI art to the next level? Getting started is entirely free:

  1. Download the App: Get it directly from the Google Play Store.
  2. Read the Guides: Visit the official web portal at aipromptbook.ni18.in to explore desktop workflows.
  3. Pick a Style: Find a category that matches your project, tap to copy, or select a photo to remix.
  4. Generate: Open your favorite AI tool, paste, and watch your idea come to life.

As an indie hacker, I build these tools because they solve problems I encounter in my own daily projects. If you enjoy using AI Prompt Book, please take a moment to leave a review on the Play Store—it helps small creators like me build and maintain free, privacy-first software.

Happy prompting!
